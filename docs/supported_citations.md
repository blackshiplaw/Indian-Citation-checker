# Supported Citation Formats

The citation extraction engine supports the following Indian legal citation patterns:

### 1. Supreme Court Cases (SCC Print)
- **Format**: `(Year) Volume SCC Page` or `Year (Volume) SCC Page`
- **Examples**:
  - `(1973) 4 SCC 225` (Kesavananda Bharati)
  - `(2017) 10 SCC 1` (Puttaswamy Right to Privacy)
  - `1997 (6) SCC 241` (Vishaka)

### 2. SCC OnLine
- **Format**: `Year SCC OnLine Court CaseNumber`
- **Examples**:
  - `2021 SCC OnLine SC 450`
  - `2018 SCC OnLine Del 12215`

### 3. All India Reporter (AIR)
- **Format**: `AIR Year Court Page`
- **Examples**:
  - `AIR 1973 SC 1461`
  - `AIR 1978 SC 597`
  - `AIR 2020 Del 120`

### 4. Supreme Court Reports (SCR)
- **Format**: `[Year] Volume SCR Page` or `(Year) Volume SCR Page`
- **Examples**:
  - `[1973] 4 SCR 541`
  - `[1978] 2 SCR 621`

### 5. Supreme Court Neutral Citations (INSC)
- **Format**: `Year INSC CaseNumber`
- **Examples**:
  - `2023 INSC 582`
  - `2017 INSC 752`
  - `2024 INSC 105`

### 6. High Court Neutral Citations
- **Format**: `Year:COURT:Number`
- **Examples**:
  - `2018:DHC:7122` (Delhi High Court)
  - `2022:BHC-OS:456` (Bombay High Court)

### 7. High Court Reporters & Specialized Journals
- **Formats**:
  - `ILR (Year) Vol Court Page` (Indian Law Reports)
  - `(Year) Vol Bom CR Page` (Bombay Cases Reporter)
  - `(Year) Vol CTC Page` (Current Tamil Nadu Cases)
  - `(Year) Vol Gau LR Page` (Gauhati Law Reports)

### 8. SCALE and Judgments Today (JT)
- **Format**: `(Year) Vol Scale Page` / `(Year) Vol JT Page`
- **Examples**:
  - `(1997) 7 Scale 384`
  - `(2002) 2 JT 450`
